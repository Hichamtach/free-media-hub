### Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [Image FX](/ai#image-generation) in Image Gen + [Video FX](/ai#video-generation) in Video Gen. AI generators made by Google; US only.

- Starred [Alu](/privacy#proxy-sites) in Proxy Sites. Proxy site similar to things like HolyUnblocker.

- Starred [Lively](/system-tools#wallpaper-managers) in Wallpaper Managers. Feature-rich live wallpaper manager for Windows.

- Starred [BandLab](/audio#browser-editors-synths) in Audio Browser Editors. Popular and feature-rich digital audio workstation.

- Starred [Kvaesitso](/mobile#app-launchers) in Android App Launchers. Feature-rich, search-focused launcher.

- Starred [SideStore](/mobile#ios-sideloading) in iOS Sideloading. Fork of AltStore that doesn't require a PC.

- Starred [uYouEnhanced](/mobile#ios-youtube-apps) in iOS YouTube apps. Modded YouTube IPA. 

- Starred [The Book of Secret Knowledge](/linux-macos#software-sites) in Linux Software. Lists, manuals, cheatsheets, tools, and more.

- Starred [You Don't Need JavaScript](/developer-tools#css) in CSS Tools. Curated list of CSS demos.
