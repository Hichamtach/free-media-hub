# Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [AnimeKai](/video) in Anime Streaming. Feature-rich, huge library, fast servers. People seem to really like it, so we've moved it to the #1 spot.

- Starred both [111477 + DramaSuki](/video#drives-directories) in Drives / Directories. Both have fast speeds and lots of content.

- Starred [BatCave](/reading#comics) in Comic Sites. New comic site with tracking, ratings, and a nice UI.

- Starred [PairDrop](/file-tools#p2p-transfer) in P2P File Transfer. Open-source, easy to use, and allows creating rooms.

- Starred [Five Books](/reading#curated-recommendations) in Book Recommendations. Expert-recommended top 5 lists with lots of categories.

- Starred [VideoGameCanon](/gaming-tools#curated-recommendations) in Game Recommendations. Game top 1,000 list that people in our server find useful.

- Starred [Mini QR](/internet-tools#url-tools) in URL Tools. Lets you create custom QR code art.

- Starred [Table Habit](/mobile#productivity-trackers) in Android Productivity Trackers. Open-source, updated regularly, and has a nice UI.

- Starred both [BGBye + Pixelcut](/storage#background-removers) in Background Removers as they seemed to work the best.

- Starred [EncycloSearch](https://encyclosearch.org/) in Encyclopedias. Combines two dozen encyclopedias into one search engine.

- Starred [Exact Audio Copy](/audio#audio-ripping-tools) in Audio Ripping. Popular CD ripping tool, recommended in Redacted's interview guide.

- Switched to [AI Studio](/ai#online-chatbots) as Google's main chatbot as it works well on mobile, is less laggy, has chat history, and has less censorship than Gemini.

- Added [Free-Official-YouTube-Content](/video#free-w-ads) as an index emoji in Free w/ Ads. Not a star, but we feel it's good enough to mention.
