- Starred [LanguageTool](/text-tools#grammar-check) in Grammar Check. This seemed to pick up the most errors when compared to other options.

- Starred [BlockAway](/privacy#proxy-sites) in Proxy Sites. Popular proxy site that seems to work well for most people.

- Starred [Plutonium](/gaming-tools#multiplayer-mods) in Multiplayer Mods. The most popular COD multiplayer server mod, works with multiple games.

- Starred [webOS Dev Manager](/video#smart-tv-firestick) in Smart TV. LG TV Homebrew Installer + Guide.

- Starred [Citrus Search](/reading#academic-papers) in Academic Papers. Easily search and find similar papers for any topic.
