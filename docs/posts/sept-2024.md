### Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [Bookracy](/reading#ebooks) in Ebook Sites. Promising book site with a solid UI and community.

- Starred [Droid-ify](/mobile#foss-apks) in FOSS APKs. Open-source F-Droid client some like more than the official.

- Starred [Disblock Origin](/privacy#adblocking) in Adblocking. Removes Nitro / Boost ads from Discord.

- Starred [Sidebery](/storage#tab-managers) in Tab Managers. Useful open-source tab manager / bookmark manager.

- Starred [FastFLUX](/ai#image-generation) in AI Image Gen. Good quality and currently unlimited. 

- Starred [You](/ai#ai-search-engines) in AI Search Engines. Fast, free, unlimited AI search.

- Starred [Archive of Our Own](/reading#fanfiction-stories) in Fanfiction Sites. Popular and extensive archive of fanfiction.

- Starred [Materialgram](/social-media-tools#telegram-clients) and [AyuGram](/mobile#social-media-apps) (Android) in Telegram Clients. Simple, ad-free, feature-rich clients.

- Starred [NCS](/storage#royalty-free-music) in Royalty Free Music. Popular library for free music.

- Starred [K-9 Mail](/mobile#android-internet) in Android Internet. Open-source mobile mail client.

- Starred [Lossless Scaling](/gaming-tools#optimization-tools) in Game Optimization. Powerful upscaling with frame generation for all GPUs.

- Starred [Hotkey Cheatsheet](/system-tools#mouse-keyboard) in Keyboard Tools. Keyboard hotkeys for lots of different apps.

- Starred [Viren070's Guides](/video#stremio-tools) in Stremio Tools. Helpful guides to use Stremio.

### Things Removed

- Said goodbye to both Fmovies and Aniwave.

- Removed SusFlix as it's been down for over a week.

- Removed Lantern as its limit is low (250mb daily) and it's closed-source.

- Removed Lexica as they're removing the free tier.

- Unstarred APKPure due to their poor privacy policy and low-quality apps.

- Unstarred ROMhacking as they're going [archive-only](https://www.romhacking.net/forum/index.php?topic=39405).

- Unstarred Morphic as they will [no longer](https://i.ibb.co/sRVyVKk/image.png) be using paid models for searching.

- Unstarred sdk.vercel as it requires an account for the better models.
